Privacy Policy

Project-Base is a client-side research tool. It runs entirely in your browser and does not create an account, store a username, or track you across devices.

  • Local storage. A small discovery cache (≈300 entries) is kept in your browser so repeated lookups are instant. It contains only coordinates and terrain metrics — no personal data.
  • Upstream calls. Elevation and weather queries are sent to Open-Meteo (public, no API key). Tile requests go to NASA/JPL Solar System Treks and open basemap providers.
  • No analytics. No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts are loaded.
  • Data you provide. Any text you type into the planner or authoring notes stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

Terms of Service

By using this tool you accept the following:

  • Informational only. Terrain metrics, rankings, and mission scores are research estimates, not engineering or safety advice. Do not use them for life-critical decisions.
